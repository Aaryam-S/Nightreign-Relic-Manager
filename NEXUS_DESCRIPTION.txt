[size=5][b]Nightreign Relic Manager[/b][/size]
[b]PC save tool: batch favorite / unfavorite / sell relics, with a dry-run preview.[/b]

[size=4][b]What it does[/b][/size]
Tired of clicking through hundreds of relics? Build a rule, preview exactly what will change, then apply.
[list]
[*]Rules from four effect groups (X, Y, Z and Demerits) combined with AND / OR / NOT and parentheses, e.g. (X OR Y) AND NOT D
[*]Effect picker grouped by type (attack power, negation, resistances...), relic-only effects
[*]Limit by tier, color and Deep relics
[*]Actions: favorite, unfavorite, or sell (optionally credits Murk)
[*]Save your own presets
[/list]
It never edits, rerolls or creates relic effects and never spawns items.

[size=4][b]Safety[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Dry run first: nothing is written until you confirm
[*]Equipped relics (vessels and saved presets) and unique relics are never sold
[*]Checksum verification, a timestamped backup before every write, atomic replace, automatic restore if anything looks wrong
[*]Read-only inspect mode to confirm the tool understands your save before you use it
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install Python 3 from python.org (tick "Add python.exe to PATH", keep tcl/tk)
[*]Extract the zip anywhere and double-click run_windows.bat
[*]Close the game, back up your save, check it with the inspect step in the README, then use the app
[/list]

[size=4][b]Please read[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Editing saves can get an online account flagged.[/b] Use offline / non-ranked saves and keep your own backup. Use at your own risk.
[*]PC saves only (NR0000.sl2 / .co2)
[*]Sell values (Murk per tier) are unverified placeholders until you enter the real prices in Settings
[*]A game update can change the save format; if the inspect check looks wrong, do not apply anything
[/list]
